## Welcome to the Social Contact Data community

A forum for anyone working with social contact data: finding it, using it and putting it to work.

### 📖 What is social contact data?

Social contact data describe how people interact with one another in everyday settings. They typically include basic demographic information and details about interactions in different settings such as households, schools and workplaces. By understanding who interacts with whom, where, how long and how often, these data help researchers understand how close-contact infectious diseases can spread through populations and improve the mathematical and epidemiological models used to characterise transmission dynamics.

### 🧭 What we talk about

- Finding and comparing social contact datasets, on [socialcontactdata.org](https://socialcontactdata.org) and beyond
- Using the data: cleaning, analysis, tools and code
- Putting it to work in transmission models and other research
- Collecting new data, sharing your work and new ideas
